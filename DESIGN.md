# Design direction

Pinned by the user: Apple-like clarity, mobile-first, light and dark themes. This is an Operate surface. Warm white and deep ink, precise system typography, quiet separators, a clear blue interaction accent. A soft lavender field identifies Daniel; blue identifies the owner. No marketing hero. Agenda rows are the product.

The signature move is a continuous time rail: quiet dots for upcoming sessions, an illuminated current-time marker on conference days, and paired attendance dots that connect a session to each person. No invented live state before the event. Desktop adds a compact overview panel; mobile retains the full agenda with a bottom navigation and sticky day selector. Details enter as a focus-managed native dialog styled as a mobile sheet. Respect reduced motion.

Craft: generous outer spacing, dense meaningful inner groups; tabular times; readable contrast; minimum 44px touch controls; search and filters local to the agenda; empty, pending, offline and retry states.
